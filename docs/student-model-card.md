# Model Card — compact student–teacher, Milestone 2D

Local research prototype, 24 October 2026. Genuine normal-only student training and bounded CPU execution are demonstrated. **Real-defect accuracy and industrial usefulness remain unestablished.** No official Cashew test inference or new real-anomaly metric was performed.

**Model.** The frozen teacher reuses verified ImageNet V1 ResNet18 through layer3 (2,782,784 parameters). Its raw layer2/layer3 maps have 128 channels at 16x16 and 256 at 8x8. The independently initialized student has 112,128 trainable parameters: stride-two 3x3 convolutions 3→16→32→64→96 with ReLU, and linear 1x1 heads 64→128 and 96→256. All convolutions have biases; student uses no BatchNorm, dropout, skip path or pretrained initialization. Both networks receive full-frame RGB 128, bilinear antialiased resize, /255 and ImageNet mean/std. Teacher parameters and BatchNorm remain frozen.

**Objective and scores.** Normalize each spatial feature vector by its channel L2 norm with epsilon 1e-6; no dataset-fitted statistics. Compute channel-summed squared teacher/student differences at each layer, bilinearly align the high-level discrepancy to 16x16, and average the two maps equally. Training loss is the batch/spatial mean. Image score is the maximum raw 16x16 value; the spatial map is bilinearly resized to 128x128, align_corners=False. No pixel threshold, smoothing or per-image score normalization. The zero-norm case remains finite under the epsilon rule.

This is **EfficientAD-inspired**, not the published algorithm. The [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.14535) motivates efficient student prediction of normal features. This prototype uses ResNet18 instead of its teacher design and omits its complete loss/training scheme, auxiliary autoencoder and score-fusion pipeline. Published accuracy/latency claims do not apply. It also differs from the earlier planning-only matching-ResNet18 proposal: the owner's 2D request specifies a compact student and at most 12 epochs.

**Training.** One full experiment, `runs/cashew-student-v1`: 315 normal training images, 67 separate normal validation images, seed 42, Adam 0.001, batch 8, four Torch CPU threads, zero workers, deterministic shuffled training, maximum 12 epochs and patience 3 on strict normal validation minima. All 12 epochs completed,480 optimizer updates; best epoch 12, validation loss 0.30894924 versus initial 1.97576105. Every epoch had exact permitted membership and finite losses. Student state changed; teacher state hash was identical before/after. Inference reload matched the saved 8-image validation probe exactly and the full 67-image validation loss within 1e-6. Training success is supported by updates, gradients, state changes, isolation and reload checks, not loss decrease alone.

The accepted dataset/splits and pretrained file were reused unchanged. No download or new dependency was needed. The weight provenance (local evidence, not distributed: `docs/evidence/resnet18-provenance.json`) and [2C methods](feature-memory.md) retain dataset-derived rights limitations; BSD code licensing alone does not grant unrestricted pretrained-weight redistribution.

**Development calibration.** 68 separate normals set linear q95 threshold **0.9195413202047348**, strict greater-than. 4/68 calibration normals exceeded it (5.882%, in-sample); 4/67 validation normals exceeded it (5.970%). Validation also selected the student checkpoint, so that exceedance rate is not an independent generalization estimate. Neither subset estimates real anomaly recall.

**Artificial probes.** The fixed 16 normal-validation checkerboards from 2C were used after selection/calibration. 15/16 increased scores, 16/16 exceeded the threshold, 13/16 placed a map maximum inside the artificial mask. Synthetic-only pooled pixel AUROC 0.99615918 and AP 0.77351594 are software/development diagnostics, not real defect estimates or evidence of superiority. No further fitting or recipe adjustment followed these outcomes. Responses are broad and sometimes include ordinary object edges/background.

**Resources.** Ryzen 7 9700X, Windows AMD64, Python 3.12.15, torch 2.13.0+cpu, torchvision 0.28.0+cpu. Training/validation/checkpoint loop 59.1796 s, process CPU 234.9375 s (~3.97 logical cores average), observed peak495,120,384 bytes (472.18 MiB). Student checkpoint 453,113 bytes. Batch-one preloaded teacher+student+score/map median 6.59785 ms/p95 7.205395 ms; batch-eight median 25.57985 ms/p95 26.40468 ms. Five warmups/30 repeats, first normal validation image(s); separate already-warm decode/resize median 8.27065 ms. Startup, cold I/O, cameras and debugger pauses are excluded. These are local measurements, not production guarantees.

**Safety and limitations.** One category, seed and recipe; normal-only selection may favor overgeneralizing students. Synthetic squares are conspicuous and unrepresentative. Coarse feature maps, square resizing, ImageNet domain shift, small calibration tails and unverified physical/acquisition independence limit interpretation. Python read guards are not OS sandboxes; local hashes show consistency, not signatures. No optimizer/RNG state is saved for exact training resume. Real samples, weights and figures remain ignored/unpublished. This model is not approved for autonomous inspection, food/safety decisions or deployment.
