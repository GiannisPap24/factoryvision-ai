including relevant contributor/employer rights, and explicitly approved
`Copyright (c) 2026 GiannisPap24`. The complete [MIT License](../LICENSE)
covers original FactoryVision source and associated owned documentation and
synthetic content. Package metadata declares MIT and includes the licence file.
This records the owner's attestation; it is not an independent title investigation.

Third-party dependencies, datasets, images and pretrained/derived model assets
retain their separate terms. They are not relicensed by this grant. Dataset
and weight redistribution is not authorized by this milestone; all such assets
remain excluded. No material conflicting notice was identified in the reviewed
owned-source boundary. The source-only [public repository](https://github.com/GiannisPap24/factoryvision-ai)
was published on 2026-10-26 after owner approval and the final security/privacy review.
The exact 60-package [dependency table](milestone-4d-dependency-licenses.csv)
records the reviewed versions and notice inventory; private audit records are omitted.

4D verified the exact Streamlit 1.66.1 upstream Apache-2.0 licence, resolving its
source-licence identity; its installed wheel has no separately named licence file.
Bundled notices need more care than top-level metadata: NumPy/SciPy include GCC
runtime exceptions, Pillow includes FreeType alternatives, setuptools vendors an
LGPL-3.0 component, and torch carries a GPL-3.0 test-directory notice. These facts
do not by themselves relicense FactoryVision code. No dependency binaries are
vendored in the source release; any future bundled installer needs a separate
component/notice review. Pretrained and derived-weight distribution remains unresolved.
